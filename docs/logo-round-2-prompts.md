## 05 / Möbius S

Use case: logo-brand. Create an art-directed logo concept presentation for Satyajit Senapati, an independent senior data and AI engineer. This is a personal portfolio identity exploration, not a generic software company. Original, sophisticated, memorable silhouette with careful optical balance and generous negative space. Flat vector-style artwork with precise curves and geometry, professional identity studio quality. Landscape 3:2 canvas. Warm ivory background #f6f4ef. Main symbol large at left in deep violet #7253a5. At right, a smaller identical symbol in pale lilac #c2aff5 on a solid ink #19171f square with subtly rounded corners; below it an identical small one-color black version. Elegant clean typographic label at lower left, exact title provided below. Under the title set the name "Satyajit Senapati" in restrained modern sans serif. Lots of breathing room. No mockup photography, bevels, shadows, gradients, metallic effects, circuit traces, neural-network dots, stock AI sparkle, clipart, or ornamental detail. The small-size silhouette must remain clear. Treat this as a distinctive designer-drawn logo, not a typed letter with a circle around it.
Concept: a bold single ribbon twists through two complementary offset loops to suggest an S through negative space. A clever optical fold creates continuity from upper left to lower right, but the mark is entirely flat and one color. Two carefully placed cuts distinguish the overlapping planes without gradients. Compact asymmetric silhouette, warm curves meeting precise cut terminals. More sculptural and inventive than a standard S, but recognizably an S when examined. Avoid an infinity symbol lying sideways. Exact title: "05 / Möbius S".
